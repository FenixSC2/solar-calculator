How the numbers are worked out

  • Daily power made = panel size × sun hours × 0.8 (real panels lose about 20% to heat, wiring and angle).
  • Used in your home: plug-in systems only save money on power you use as it's made. Small systems use almost all of it (98% at 200 W); bigger ones less (60% at 1,200 W, 30% at 3,000 W). A battery saves some of the leftover for later, up to 95% total.
  • Yearly savings = power made per year × share used at home × your rate (plus the time-of-use bonus if it's on).
  • Payback = system cost ÷ first-year savings.
  • 10- and 25-year profit: panels lose 0.5% of output per year, and electricity prices rise by the yearly increase you set, starting in year 2. Minus what the system cost.
  • Typical price = $0.80 per watt of panels + $400 for the inverter, or + $0.75 per Wh of battery (battery systems include the inverter). No tax credit is applied.
  • Backup value with a battery is an estimate of what riding out power outages is worth ($35 per outage hour covered). It's not bill savings, so it's shown separately.

This is an estimate, not a quote. Battery replacement costs aren't included. Check your utility's and state's rules before installing. Formulas follow the calculator at pluginsolarus.com.
